## The Emerald Tablet

1. It is, without falsehood, certain and most true.

2. What is above is like what is below, and what is below is like what is above, to accomplish the wonders of the One Thing.

3. And as all things came from One, through the mediation of One, so all things were born from this One Thing by adaptation.

4. Its father is the Sun, its mother is the Moon.

5. The wind carried it in its womb, and the earth is its nurse.

6. It is the source of every work of wonder in the world. Its power is complete.

7. When it is turned toward the earth, it separates earth from fire, the subtle from the gross.

8. With great strength it ascends from earth to heaven. Then it descends again to earth, receiving the power of both the higher and the lower realms.

9. Thus you will receive the glory of the whole world, and all darkness will flee from you.

10. This is the strongest of all strength, for it overcomes every subtle thing and penetrates every solid thing.

11. Thus was the world created.

12. From this arise marvelous transformations, and this is the method by which they are accomplished.

13. Therefore I am called Hermes Trismegistus, because I possess the three parts of the wisdom of the whole world.

14. And now what I have said concerning the work of the Sun is complete.
